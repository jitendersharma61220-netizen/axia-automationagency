The engine, stage by stage

  1. 01

    Research

    Reads the company website, recent news and the contact's role so every message starts from real context.

  2. 02

    Score fit

    Scores each lead against your ideal customer and puts the best ones first.

  3. 03

    Write outreach

    Drafts a personal first message for WhatsApp or email, in Hindi or English, with real free meeting slots.

  4. 04

    You approve

    Your rep reviews the batch in one place and approves, edits or skips each message.

  5. 05

    Send and book

    Sends, follows up on silence, sorts every reply into interested, later or not now, and books meetings.
