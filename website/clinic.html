Where the hours go today

The engine, stage by stage

  1. 01

    Draft notes

    Turns the consultation into structured notes and a draft prescription.

  2. 02

    Safety check

    Flags allergies, duplicate medicines and missing doses before the doctor sees the draft.

  3. 03

    Doctor approves

    Nothing reaches a patient until the doctor reviews and signs it.

  4. 04

    Explain

    Explains lab reports in simple language the patient understands, with critical values sent to the doctor first.

  5. 05

    Remind

    Sends dose reminders, follow-up visit reminders and repeat test alerts on WhatsApp.
